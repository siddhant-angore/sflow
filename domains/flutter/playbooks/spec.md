- **In** "Interview per the **grilling** skill": Before the first round, spawn `subagent_type: "sflow-flutter:user-flow-analysis-agent"` to map the user flows and their permutations; its gaps become interview questions. Ask the WCAG level and the target platforms once, per the **vgv-accessibility** skill.
- **In** "Break it into tickets per the **to-tickets** skill": Every acceptance criterion carries a `verify:` line, a command or `manual`, in the success-criteria format of the **wingspan-plan** skill. A ticket that adds a layer or a package names it per the **layered-architecture** skill, and `subagent_type: "sflow-flutter:plan-splitting-agent"` decides whether it splits.
