### Writing

Code-adjacent docs (README, RFC, ADR, PR body) use the **technical-writing** and **unslop** skills instead.

1. Explore with the **writing-fragments** skill, then exploit with the **writing-beats** and **writing-shape** skills, one paragraph at a time.
2. A plan for non-code workflows is grilled per the **loop-me** skill. A non-repo idea is grilled per the **grill-me** skill. Teaching material follows the **teach-workspace** skill.
3. Finish with the **unslop** skill, then the **wait-what** skill if the reader says it did not land.
