- **Before** "Rebase into small, ordered commits before opening PRs": Sensors before judges: the project's checks (the `gate` in `.harness.json`, or the repo's lint, typecheck, and tests) pass before any reviewer is spawned, so judge tokens are never spent on code a test would reject. Then the review fan-out, depth one, in parallel, per `<sflow>/references/orchestration-patterns.md`. Skip it only when the diff is five files or fewer, under two hundred lines, and touches no auth, input-parsing, file-path, shell, network, dependency, or config path. Otherwise spawn `subagent_type: "sflow:code-reviewer"` and `subagent_type: "sflow:test-engineer"`, plus `subagent_type: "sflow:security-auditor"` when the diff touches a trust boundary, each given the diff range and the task, never your summary, and told not to edit files. Add a finder that reads the **code-review** skill and runs only its Spec axis against the task. After the reports return, run the mutation experiment from the section Verifying Tests in the **code-review-and-quality** skill: invert one condition the change relies on; a green suite means a missing test. Then walk the rows of `<sflow>/references/definition-of-done.md` and `<sflow>/references/security-checklist.md` that the diff implicates; a UI change also `accessibility-checklist.md`, a perf change `performance-checklist.md`, a change that adds a service or a job `observability-checklist.md`. Every Required or Critical finding is fixed or dismissed with a concrete reason; skipped checklist items go in the decision trail (the **show-me-your-work** skill).
- **In** "`## Scope`": Add one line for the door type, one-way or two-way, per the **pr** skill, and the THINGS I DIDN'T TOUCH and POTENTIAL CONCERNS items from the change summary in the **git-workflow-and-versioning** skill.
- **In** "`## Verification`": A change that alters what runs in production links a rollout note (thresholds and rollback per the **shipping-and-launch** skill, the two to four on-call questions and their signals per the **observability-and-instrumentation** skill) kept beside the decision trail, not in the body. A decision worth an ADR is written per the **documentation-and-adrs** skill in the same PR. A CI change follows the **ci-cd-and-automation** skill: no gate can be skipped.
